## PASO 2 · Fijar el escenario (1 imagen, 4:3)
Sin personas. Sirve de referencia de fondo en todos los planos.
```
Empty outdoor basketball court inside a tall chain-link cage, the floor painted in bold colour blocks (red, emerald green, caramel, lilac, sky blue) with white court lines, one glass backboard hoop with a chain net, city apartment blocks behind, bright late-afternoon sun, long crisp shadows. Paint chipping, surface grain noise, metal oxidation on the fence. No people, no text, no logos.
```

## PASO 3 · Fotogramas de inicio para Seedance (6 imágenes, 4:3)
Cada uno será el primer fotograma de un clip. Adjuntar siempre la hoja del paso 1 del personaje que sale y el escenario del paso 2.

**K1 · Cordones** (plano 1). Adjuntar: hoja B.
```
Extreme close-up of the young woman's burgundy manicured hands pulling tight the laces of her black-and-gold sneaker on the colour-blocked court floor, gold outline and snakeskin AIR letters in sharp focus, red painted floor block.
```
**K2 · Él la subestima y ella sonríe** (plano 3). Adjuntar: hojas A y B + escenario.
```
Medium shot: the young man spins a basketball on one finger with a cocky half smile, in the lilac court zone; behind him, slightly out of focus, the young woman bites her lower lip and smiles, arms crossed, sizing him up.
```
**K3 · El cruce** (plano 6). Adjuntar: hojas A y B + escenario.
```
Low side angle, mid crossover: the young woman dribbles the ball low between her legs, eyes locked on the young man, who leans the wrong way off balance, caramel court zone, motion blur on the ball.
```
**K4 · El mate** (planos 10-11). Adjuntar: hoja B + escenario.
```
Extreme low angle from under the hoop: the young woman mid-air dunking with both hands, hair flying, the glass backboard shattering into hundreds of colourful shards (red, green, lilac, gold) frozen in the air around her. Real glass distortion, subtle refraction, chromatic dispersion, micro reflections.
```
**K5 · Beso a cámara** (plano 11). Adjuntar: hoja B + escenario.
```
Medium close-up: the young woman just landed, glass shards raining around her in slow motion, she fixes her hair with one hand and blows a kiss straight to the camera, sweat on her skin, powerful and playful, golden light.
```
**K6 · El guiño y la mano** (plano 12). Adjuntar: hojas A y B + escenario.
```
High angle: the young man sitting on the court floor laughing among glass shards; the young woman stands over him, winks and offers her hand to pull him up, lilac court zone.
```
✅ Revisa en cada fotograma: cara de cada uno, zapatillas no intercambiadas, manos con 5 dedos y nada de texto.

## PASO 4 · Seedance (lo preparo yo cuando tengas el paso 3)
Cada fotograma K → clip de 3-5 s con **un solo** movimiento de cámara (tabla del tratamiento v2). Los planos sin fotograma (dron, balón-cámara, valla, carrera) parten de texto con las hojas A/B y el escenario como referencia.
