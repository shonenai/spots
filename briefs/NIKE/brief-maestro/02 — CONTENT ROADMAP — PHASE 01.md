# NIKE AI — CONTENT ROADMAP

## PHASE 01 — CHARACTER ESTABLISHMENT

Antes de vender el producto tenemos que presentar a las personas.

### CONTENT

1. Character A portrait
2. Character B portrait
3. Full-body Character A
4. Full-body Character B
5. Character A basketball
6. Character B basketball
7. Character A lifestyle
8. Character B lifestyle

### OBJECTIVE

Establecer identidad visual.

---

# PHASE 02 — THE OUTFIT

Introducir Nike apparel.

### CONTENT

- Outfit photos
- Mirror UGC
- Sneaker close-ups
- Getting dressed
- Before training
- Streetwear
- Full-body editorial

### VIDEO

"Get ready with me."

"Training outfit."

"What I wear to play basketball."

---

# PHASE 03 — THE COURT

Introducir el basketball como elemento principal.

### CONTENT

- Walking onto the court
- Ball close-up
- Sneakers touching court
- Dribbling
- Running
- Jump shot
- Defense
- Two-character interaction

### VIDEO

15–20 second basketball sequence.

---

# PHASE 04 — THE GAME

Aumentar la intensidad.

### STRUCTURE

START

Silence.

Close-up.

Shoes.

Ball.

Hands.

BREATHING.

Then:

RUN.

DRIBBLE.

JUMP.

SHOOT.

LAND.

CUT.

Final shot.

---

# PHASE 05 — UGC

Una vez establecidos los personajes, crear contenido aparentemente espontáneo.

### EXAMPLES

**"Come with me to basketball training."**

**"Today's fit."**

**"Testing these for the first time."**

**"POV: you said you were only going to shoot around."**

**"Training day."**

**"Post-game."**

---

# PHASE 06 — HERO CAMPAIGN

Finalmente unir todos los elementos.

### HERO FILM

Dos personajes.

Una cancha.

Una ciudad.

Un producto.

Una historia.

Un objetivo.

La pieza debe combinar:

- Character
- Product
- Basketball
- Fashion
- Cinematic movement
- Sound design
- Social storytelling

---

# PHASE 07 — CONTENT RECYCLING

Cada producción debe generar múltiples piezas.

Por ejemplo:

## UNA SESIÓN DE BASKETBALL

Puede producir:

### VIDEO

1 Hero film

2 Reel

3 TikTok

4 YouTube Short

### PHOTOGRAPHY

1 Hero image

2 Portrait

3 Product image

4 Action image

5 Detail image

### UGC

1 Training vlog

2 Outfit check

3 Sneaker review

4 Behind the scenes

Así conseguimos que una única producción genere una gran cantidad de contenido.

---

# PRODUCTION PRINCIPLE

No crear:

**1 prompt → 1 publicación**

Crear:

**1 CONCEPT → múltiples assets**

Ejemplo:

BASKETBALL NIGHT

↓

Hero Film

↓

Action Photos

↓

Sneaker Photos

↓

UGC

↓

Behind the Scenes

↓

Instagram Carousel

↓

TikTok

↓

YouTube Short

↓

Stories

El objetivo es pensar como una productora, no como alguien generando imágenes individuales.
